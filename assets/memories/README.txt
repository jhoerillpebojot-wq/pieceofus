PUT YOUR PHOTOS HERE
====================
One folder per chapter:
  chapter-1  Our Beginning          chapter-4  My Favorite Person
  chapter-2  Little Moments         chapter-5  Memories I Treasure
  chapter-3  Our Adventures         chapter-6  More Memories to Come
  special    A Special Collection

1. Copy a photo (.jpg, .jpeg, .png or .webp) into the chapter folder.
2. Open js/album-config.js, find the memory, and set its photo field to the file name, e.g. photo: "your-file-name.jpg".
3. Bump VERSION in sw.js whenever bundled photos or app files change so installed phones download the update.

Tips: photos around 1200-1600 px on the long side look sharp and stay reasonably small.
Use simple file names (letters, numbers, dashes). Photos are never stretched or cropped in the viewer.
